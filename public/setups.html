Understeer: the car pushes wide, it turns less than you asked.
Apex: the middle of the corner, closest to the inside.
Brake point: where you first brake before a corner. Later = faster, until you miss it.
Downforce: wings pressing the car down. More wing = more corner grip, less straight speed.
Differential: how the rear wheels are linked. High % = stable, pushes wide. Low % = rotates, can snap.
Anti-roll bar: resists leaning. Stiffer front = pushes wide. Stiffer rear = looser back.
